Bucktail Privacy Policy

Bucktail: A&P Shop Toolkit, an iOS app by Cloven & Works Private Limited. Effective 23 November 2026.

Bucktail does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your device

Bucktail keeps only two things, in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • the last few part numbers you decoded, so you can tap them again, and
  • whether Bucktail Pro is unlocked.

We cannot see this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backs up apps to iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup includes it under Apple's own privacy terms.

Purchases

Bucktail Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether Pro is unlocked. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Network

The app works fully offline. Its only network traffic is the App Store purchase and restore requests made through Apple, and opening this website in your browser if you tap Support.

Children

Bucktail is a professional reference. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
