Wheelhouse Privacy Policy

Wheelhouse: Captain License, an iOS app by Cloven & Works Private Limited. Effective 23 November 2026.

Wheelhouse does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or do in Wheelhouse is saved only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage:

  • your license goal and exam date,
  • your vessels, sea-time log and application checklist,
  • your study progress, practice-exam results and trainer scores,
  • your display and study settings.

We cannot see this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If your device backs up apps to iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup includes it under Apple's own privacy terms.

Sharing a sea-service PDF

When you export the CG-719S worksheet, Wheelhouse makes a PDF on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Wheelhouse Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details or personal information. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple ID.

Network

The app works fully offline. Its only network traffic is the App Store purchase and restore requests made through Apple.

Children

Wheelhouse is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
