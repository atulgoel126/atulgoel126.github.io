VINspect Privacy Policy

VINspect: Used Car VIN Check, an iOS app by Cloven & Works Private Limited. Effective 27 November 2026.

VINspect does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not receive anything you type or scan.

Looking up a car

To build a report, the app sends the vehicle identification number (VIN), and the year, make and model it decodes to, directly from your iPhone to these U.S. government services:

  • NHTSA (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov and api.nhtsa.gov), for decoding the VIN, recalls, owner complaints and crash test ratings, and
  • the EPA (fueleconomy.gov), for fuel economy.

These requests go straight to the government services and are covered by their own privacy policies. They do not pass through us.

What stays on your iPhone

Your recent checks, saved cars and checklist ticks are kept only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them, and deleting the app deletes them. If your iPhone backs up apps to iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup includes them under Apple's own privacy terms.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a VIN barcode. Images are processed on your iPhone and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Subscriptions

VINspect Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment and tells the app whether your subscription is active. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Children

VINspect is a tool for people buying used cars. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
